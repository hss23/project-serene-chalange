# Community Code of Conduct

## Our expectations
All participants are expected to be respectful, punctual and honest. Mentors share their experience as personal perspective, not as professional advice, and mentees decide for themselves what to act on. Discrimination, harassment and pressure of any kind are not tolerated.

## Confidentiality
What is discussed in a session stays between the mentor and mentee unless both agree to share it. The exception is when someone's safety is at risk, in which case coordinators must be informed. Mentors must not share a mentee's work samples, salary details or job search status with anyone else.

## Boundaries
The programme is about career development. Mentors are not therapists, recruiters, lawyers or financial advisers, and should point mentees to appropriate professional services when a topic is outside career guidance. Mentors must not offer jobs to their mentees during an active mentorship without telling the coordinators, to avoid conflicts of interest.

## Reporting a concern
Concerns can be reported confidentially through the "Report a concern" form in the dashboard or by emailing the coordinators. Every report is acknowledged within two working days. The coordinators may pause a mentorship while a report is reviewed.

## Consequences
Breaking the code of conduct can lead to a warning, the end of a mentorship, or removal from the programme, depending on how serious the issue is. Decisions can be appealed once by replying to the decision email within 14 days.
