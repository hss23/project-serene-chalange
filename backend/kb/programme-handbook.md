# Programme Handbook

## About the Serene Mentorship Programme
The Serene Mentorship Programme is a fictional, volunteer-run initiative that pairs early- and mid-career professionals (mentees) with experienced practitioners (mentors) in technology, design, data, product and business roles. The programme is free for both mentors and mentees and runs entirely online, with optional in-person meetups in a few cities.

The programme's mission is to make career guidance accessible to people who do not have senior professionals in their personal network.

## Programme cycles and duration
Each mentorship runs for one cycle of 12 weeks. There are three cycles per year, starting in January, May and September. Applications for a cycle open six weeks before it starts and close two weeks before the start date.

At the end of a cycle, a pair may agree to continue for one additional cycle. After two consecutive cycles the mentee is encouraged to find a new mentor so they can gain a different perspective.

## Time commitment
Mentors and mentees are expected to meet for at least 45 minutes every two weeks, which means a minimum of six sessions per cycle. Most pairs meet weekly for 30 to 60 minutes. Mentees should plan around two hours per week in total, including preparation and follow-up tasks. Mentors should plan around one to two hours per week.

## Eligibility
Mentees must be at least 18 years old and either working in, studying for, or transitioning into a role covered by the programme. Mentors need at least five years of professional experience in their field. Mentors may support a maximum of four mentees at the same time, and each mentor sets their own lower limit in their profile.

## Leaving the programme early
Either party can end a mentorship at any time by using the "End mentorship" option in the dashboard or by emailing the programme coordinators. No reason is required. Coordinators will offer the mentee a new match in the same cycle if one is available.
