# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does the programme cost anything?
No. The programme is free for mentors and mentees. It is run by volunteers and funded by small grants from fictional partner organisations. Participants will never be asked for payment details.

## Can I have more than one mentor?
A mentee can have only one active mentor per cycle. This keeps the mentor's advice consistent and leaves enough mentors for everyone. Mentees can join group circles hosted by other mentors.

## How do I become a mentor?
Apply through the "Become a mentor" page. Applicants need at least five years of professional experience, complete a 30-minute onboarding call, and read the code of conduct. New mentors are paired with an experienced "buddy mentor" for their first cycle.

## What happens to my data?
Profile and matching data are stored to run the matching service. Chat history and saved matches are private to your account. You can delete your saved matches at any time, and you can ask the coordinators to delete your account and profile completely.

## What if my mentor stops responding?
If your mentor has not replied for ten days, contact the coordinators. They will reach out to the mentor and, if needed, offer you a new match within the same cycle.

## Is there a certificate?
Yes. Mentees who attend at least six sessions and submit the end-of-cycle feedback form receive a digital certificate of participation. Mentors receive a certificate of contribution after each cycle.
