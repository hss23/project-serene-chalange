# Session Formats

## One-to-one sessions
The core of the programme is a one-to-one video call between a mentor and a mentee. Sessions are usually 30 to 60 minutes long. The mentee owns the agenda and should share it with the mentor at least 24 hours before the session.

## Group circles
Once per cycle, mentors may host a group circle of up to six mentees. Circles last 90 minutes and focus on a single theme such as interviewing, giving presentations or negotiating a promotion. Circles are optional and are announced in the community calendar two weeks in advance.

## In-person and hybrid meetups
Pairs who live in the same city may choose to meet in person in a public place such as a café or co-working space. Hybrid pairs alternate between in-person and online sessions. The programme does not reimburse travel costs, and online sessions are always an acceptable alternative.

## Rescheduling and cancellations
Sessions should be rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance. If a mentee misses two sessions in a row without notice, the mentor may ask the coordinators to close the mentorship. Mentors who need to pause for more than two weeks should tell their mentee and the coordinators.

## Session notes
Mentees are asked to write a short summary after each session with three parts: what was discussed, what they will try next, and any questions for next time. Notes are private to the pair unless the mentee chooses to share them with coordinators.
